# ELANCO Research Notes

- **Elanco** is a global leader in animal health care, serving over **90 countries**.  
  Established in **1954** (over 70 years old now).  
  Though they are a company for all animals, they specifically focus on **five core species**:  
  **Dog, Cat, Cattle, Swine, and Poultry**.

- **Products and Brands**  
  They have **over 16 manufacturing sites** and **over 200 other brands** that aren't just for pets but also for farmers and farm animals.

- **Website Overview**  
  Their website is a basic standard website with an "okay" amount of interaction (though mostly informational).  
  It isn't super pleasing to the eye and uses **white** as the primary colour with a **solid blue** colour as their accent colour.

- **Main Company Values**
  - **Integrity** → Do the right thing the right way.
  - **Respect** → Respect for people, our customers and the animals in their care.
  - **Excellence** → Be accountable. Continuously improve. Deliver with disiple.
